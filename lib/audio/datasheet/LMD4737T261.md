## **MEMS Microphone**

### 1. Introduction

The LMD4737T MEMS Microphones are integrated with specialized Pre-amplification ASIC to provide high sensitivity, high SNR output from a capacitive audio sensor. It’s packaged for

surface mounting and high temperature reflow assembly. Data format is a single bit PDM.

### 10. Reliability Test

The samples should be placed in the room with 23+/-2 ℃ , 55+/-10%R.H. for 2 hours at least before final measurement, unless otherwise specified.

|Item|Detail|Standard|
|---|---|---|
|Simulated<br>Reflow<br>(Without<br>Solder)|Samples for qualification testing require 3 times 260±5℃<br>reflow solder profiles. 2 hours of setting time is required<br>between each reflow profile test.|±3 dB|
|Static<br>Humidity|Precondition at +25℃for 1 hour. Then expose to +85℃<br>with 85% relative humidityfor 240 hours.|±3 dB|
|Temperature<br>Shock|Each cycle shall consist of 30 minutes at -40℃, 30 minutes<br>at +125℃with 5 minutes transition time. Test duration is for<br>30 cycles, startingfrom cold to hot temperature.|±3 dB|
|ESD<br>Sensitivity|According to MIL-STD-883G, Method 3015.7 for Human<br>Body Model.<br>Discharge Position:    I/O pins<br>Charge Voltage:      ±3000V<br>Discharge Network:   100pF & 1500Ω|±3 dB|
|Random<br>Vibrations|Vibrate randomly along three perpendicular directions for 30<br>minutes in each direction, 4 cycles from 20Hz~2000Hz with<br>apeak acceleration 20g.|±3 dB|
|Mechanical<br>Shock|Subject samples to half sine shock pulses (3000g±15% for<br>0.3ms)in each direction, totally18 shocks.|±3 dB|
|High<br>temperature<br>Storage|Microphone unit must maintain sensitivity after storage at<br>+105℃for 240 hours.|±3 dB|
|Low<br>temperature<br>Storage|Microphone unit must maintain sensitivity after storage at<br>–40℃for 240 hours.|±3 dB|
|Drop Test|The test was repeated in six directions for 3 times, Dropped<br>from 1.5m height on to a steel surface, total 18 times and<br>inspected for mechanical damage.|±3 dB|
